theSouthline Edit Style

The house rules for every video I edit for you: typography, colour, captions, cuts, zooms, pacing, motion graphics and sound. It is learned from two reference reels and deliberately built on its own choices, so the edits feel related to them without copying either one.

01 · Colour

Two neutrals, one accent

Every frame is mostly neutral. Cobalt appears on one element per frame at most: the stressed word's underline, a line, a single shape. Footage and photos are graded close to monochrome so the accent always wins.

  • GradePhotos and footage: saturation 0–20 %, contrast +15, blacks lifted to Carbon (never pure #000). Skin may keep 20 % saturation on talking heads.
  • ScenesAlternate Cloud and Carbon scenes to mark shifts in the story (problem on Carbon, solution on Cloud, end card on Carbon).
  • LightOne soft cobalt haze per scene at 8–12 % opacity, drifting slowly. Never multi-colour glow blobs.
02 · Typography

A whispered lead, a loud key word

Two voices in every caption: a small serif-italic lead-in that sets up the thought, and a tight bold grotesk key word that lands it. A mono face handles labels and annotations only.

  • Key wordInstrument Sans Bold, tracking −4 %, line-height 0.95, lowercase or sentence case. 150–190 px at 1080 wide.
  • Lead-inInstrument Serif Italic at 0.42× the key word (64–80 px), 75 % opacity, sits directly above the key word, left-aligned with it.
  • Body / UIInstrument Sans Medium 40–48 px for lists, cards and lower thirds.
  • AnnotationIBM Plex Mono 22–26 px, uppercase, +12 % tracking, Graphite. Scene numbers, timecodes, labels.
  • NeverAll-caps headlines, drop shadows, outlines on readable text, more than two weights in one frame.
03 · Captions

Word by word, on the voice

  • SyncEach word appears on its spoken syllable, transcribed with Whisper. Words rise 24 px from an 8 px blur in 180 ms, expo-out.
  • HighlightThe stressed word gets a cobalt underline that sweeps in left to right (220 ms) as it is spoken. One highlight per caption.
  • ChunkingMax 3 words in the key line, max 4 in the lead-in. Break on meaning, never mid-phrase.
  • PositionUpper-middle band, 30–45 % down the frame, 72 px side margins, left-aligned. Never in the bottom 20 % (platform UI).
  • ExitPrevious caption leaves in 120 ms (fade + 12 px up) as the next lead-in starts. No overlap of two captions.

04 · Cuts and pacing

Something changes every half second

Reference 2 averaged a new shot about every 1.8 s; reference 1 never stood still. Our edits keep a shot 1.2–2.2 s and add a smaller change (a word, a push, a mark) on every eighth note of the music.

  • HookMotion in the very first frame; the core claim is readable by 1.0 s. No logo, no slow fade-in.
  • Cut pointsCut on a beat or on the first syllable of a new phrase. Hard cuts for new ideas; morphs when the same object continues.
  • Length12–25 s for ads and reels. Last 1.5–2 s is a calm end card that loops cleanly back to frame one.
  • BreathersOne slower beat (2–2.5 s, single element, no new text) before the payoff.
05 · Camera, zoom and transitions

Always drifting, never shaking

  • DriftEvery shot pushes in 3–6 % over its length (ease in-out). Static frames are not allowed.
  • Punch-in12 % scale punch in 250 ms on the key word or reveal, expo-out, then continue drifting.
  • SignatureCobalt line wipe: a 6 px cobalt line crosses the frame (400 ms, ease in-out) and the next scene follows behind it. Use at most twice per edit.
  • MorphSame-object continuity: a word becomes a bar, a bar becomes a card, a card becomes the end card. 450 ms, back-out 1.4.
  • Zoom-throughPush into a dark letter counter or a negative space to enter the next scene (300 ms ease-in, then 400 ms expo-out).
  • EasingEntrances expo-out 0.5 s · exits power-in 0.25 s · camera ease in-out · UI cards back-out 1.4 · never linear except particles and grain.
  • ShakeOnly on an impact (a stamp, a slam): max 8 px, 200 ms.

06 · Motion graphics

Measure marks, not stickers

Our graphic language comes from technical drawings and tape measures: thin rules, crop corners, tick rulers and small mono labels that frame the subject. It replaces the circles, rings and sticker clutter of the references.

  • MarksCrop corners (14 px arms, 1.5 px Fog or Graphite), tick rulers, hairline rules. They draw on in 300 ms when a scene opens.
  • LabelsMono annotations in corners: scene number and role ("02 — PROBLEM"), a timecode, or a measured fact.
  • Ghost wordThe key idea set huge (600–900 px) as a 1 px outline at 10–12 % opacity behind the subject, sliding 60 px across the shot.
  • ShapesCobalt bars and rectangles only (radius 4–8 px). No circles, suns, rings or stars.
  • CutoutsMonochrome subject cut-outs on Cloud with a soft contact shadow; one cobalt bar behind them for depth.
  • DataNumbers count up (power-out, 0.8–1.2 s) in tabular figures; one hero number per frame.
  • UI cardsCarbon cards, 32 px radius, a single cobalt detail; tilt up to 8° in 3D when they enter, settle flat.
07 · Music and sound

The voice leads, the beat keeps time

  • MusicMinimal electronic or tech-house, 118–128 BPM, warm sub, sparse top end, no vocals under the voiceover. Licensed or royalty-free only.
  • VoiceoverClose, dry, conversational. Cleaned (de-noise, light compression), centred.
  • MixFinal −14 LUFS integrated, true peak −1 dBTP. Music ducks 8–10 dB under speech with a 150 ms release.
  • SFX paletteAir whoosh on transitions · soft sub-thump on the hook and impacts · faint tick on word reveals (−28 dB) · short riser into the end card · one resolved hit to finish.
  • SyncCuts and punch-ins land on beats; caption words land on syllables. If they disagree, the voice wins.
08 · Inspired, not copied

Originality rules

The references taught the principles: restraint, one accent, two-voice captions, continuous motion, and voice-led pacing. Everything you see in our edits must be our own.

Always

  • Our palette, our three typefaces, our measure-mark graphics
  • Your own footage, licensed stock, or graphics we build
  • Original copy written for your audience
  • A side-by-side check against the references before each render: if a frame could pass for theirs, change it

Never

  • The red circle or sun motif, dotted rings, QR or barcode decoration
  • Apple or any other brand's logo, UI or product imagery
  • Film or TV stills, celebrities or animated characters we don't have rights to
  • The references' exact layouts, caption wording, music or sequence of shots
09 · Before every render

Pre-flight checklist

  1. Hook moves in frame one and reads by 1.0 s.
  2. Only Cloud, Carbon, Graphite, Fog and one Cobalt element per frame.
  3. Captions: serif-italic lead, bold key line of 3 words max, one cobalt underline, synced to the voice.
  4. No text or key visual in the bottom 20 % of the frame.
  5. Every shot drifts; shots run 1.2–2.2 s; something changes every half second.
  6. Cobalt line wipe used no more than twice.
  7. Mix at −14 LUFS, true peak −1 dBTP, music ducked under speech.
  8. Originality check against the references passed.
  9. End card 1.5–2 s, loops back cleanly to the first frame.
