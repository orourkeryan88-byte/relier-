# Research: Irish electricians and getting work (Nov 2026)

Source quality varies; most lead-platform figures come from rival platforms' marketing pages. Use these to shape the
angle; don't put the numbers on screen.

## Problems
- **Shared leads**: Irish lead sites reportedly sell the same enquiry to up to 4–5 tradespeople (Tradesmen.ie, Bark);
  credits ~€5, leads €10 to €125+; complaints of fake leads, price shoppers, a "race to the bottom", auto-renewals.
  Shared leads convert ~10–20 % (US figure). Customers go quiet after collecting quotes.
- **Big jobs go to whoever's found first**: SEAI grant changes (solar PV €1,800 opened from 6 Oct 2026 to homes
  built before 2025; reported €600 battery grant; heat-pump grants, figures disputed). One installer blog reports
  6–14 week solar waits from a shortage of qualified electricians (unverified). Grant work needs SEAI registration
  per scheme, not just Safe Electric.
- **Feast or famine**: quieter mid-winter/mid-summer; dependence on word of mouth or a builder.
- **No time**: up a ladder/under a floor when the phone rings; quoting in the evenings.
- Rates context: ~€50–80/h, day rate ~€280–500, call-out €60–100 (varies by source).

## Language that lands
Sparks, lads, the van, a small job for a neighbour, pricing jobs at the kitchen table, worth the drive, Safe Electric
registered, grant work, rewire, fuse board, EV charger, solar and battery.
