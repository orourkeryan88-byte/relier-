# Script framework

## Beats (20–26 s)
| Beat | Time | Job | Example (ad #6) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Hook | 0–2.5 s | Call out the trade + a claim they feel; motion in frame 1 | "Sparks… people all over Ireland need an electrician right now." |
| Open loop | ~2 s | A question the ad answers later | "So why are you fighting for scraps?" |
| Relate / problem | 4–6 s | Their real situation, concrete and visual | "You pay for a lead… four other lads get it too, and the cheapest wins." |
| Stakes | 3–5 s | What they lose | "And the big jobs (solar, batteries, rewires) go to whoever they find first." |
| Solution | 4–6 s | Southline in plain words | "Southline runs ads for your business. Your own leads… checked before your phone even rings." |
| Payoff | ~2 s | The breather, one idea | "You only quote the jobs worth the drive." |
| CTA | ~3 s | Comment <KEYWORD> + what happens | "Comment LEADS, and we'll show you how." |

Word budget: ~3 words per second of VO. Aim for 70–80 words at 24 s.

## Captions
Lead-in (serif italic, ≤4 words) over a key line (bold, ≤3 words), word-by-word on the VO timings, one gold underline
on the stressed word, band 30–45 % down the frame, nothing in the bottom 20 %. Caption text should be the spoken words
(trim fillers like "even"); don't caption words that aren't said.

## When the user asks for a *video prompt* (text deliverable)
Give, in this order: 1) the problem (bullets with sources and caveats), 2) the solution (Southline), 3) how to relate
(language bank), 4) a paste-ready prompt in a ```text block:

```text
PROJECT: Southline. Meta ad for <audience>. 9:16, 1080x1920, <runtime>s, sound-on with captions.
GOAL: Get <audience> to comment "<KEYWORD>" to learn how Southline …
AUDIENCE: …
ANGLE: …
STYLE (Southline Edit Style v2): palette, type, captions, motion, graphics, footage, sound (copy from the brand sheet)
VOICEOVER: Irish, 30s–40s, plain and conversational, like a mate in the trade.
SCRIPT / SHOTS: numbered beats with time ranges, VO line, caption lead/key, visual, sound cue
RULES: no other brands' logos or UI; no invented results or numbers on screen; bottom 20 % clear; original or licensed footage.
```
5) AI B-roll prompts (text can't be rendered reliably by video models; add captions in the edit), 6) what to verify.
Check the VO word count against the runtime before handing it over.
