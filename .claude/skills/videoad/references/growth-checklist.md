# Growth checklist (the owner's rule for every video)

The owner's reference, kept as a standing rule: *"Without marketing, no one sees you. Without branding, no one chooses
you. Without design, no one trusts you. Without content, no one hears you. Without distribution, no one finds you.
Without automation, you can't scale. Without innovation, you never stand out. Without proof, no one believes you.
Growth needs all the above."*

Run this checklist on every script and every finished cut. Each line names what the video must do.

| Without… | …no one / you can't | What every ad must have |
|---|---|---|
| Marketing | sees you | A frame-1 hook that stops the scroll: the audience callout or the pain, readable on the first frame. |
| Branding | chooses you | One recognisable look: the owner's end card (SA for clothing, Southline for trades), consistent type and a signature motif per ad. Name who we are. |
| Design | trusts you | Clean, premium layout: big readable text, nothing overlapping, nothing in the Reels UI zones, one accent per frame. |
| Content | hears you | One clear story in full sentences: who it's for, their problem in their words, what it costs them, what we do, the payoff. |
| Distribution | finds you | Built for where it runs: 9:16, sound-off captions, music and SFX-only cuts, a post caption, 20–25 s. Suggest placements and audiences when relevant. |
| Automation | can't scale | A comment keyword that a DM automation answers, shown mid-video and on the end card, with a real reason to comment (a free plan, audit or checklist). |
| Innovation | never stand out | A new angle, hook, visual system, colours and keyword each time (check the angles log). |
| Proof | believes you | Show something real: the owner on camera, a real result, a testimonial or a screenshot (with permission). Illustrative numbers are not proof, so say they're examples. When no proof exists yet, ask the owner for it and flag the gap in the delivery note. |

**Before delivering, score the ad 0–1 on each row.** If proof or automation scores 0, say so and say what's needed.
