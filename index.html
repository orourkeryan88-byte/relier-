FAQ

Questions we get asked

Do you really operate 24 hours a day?

Yes. We take emergency call-outs around the clock, including weekends and bank holidays. For urgent work such as burst pipes, storm damage or lock-outs, call 085 865 1313 and you will speak to someone directly.

Which areas of Dublin do you cover?

We cover Dublin city and county along with the whole of Fingal, including Swords, Malahide, Portmarnock, Howth, Balbriggan, Skerries, Rush, Lusk, Donabate, Blanchardstown and Castleknock.

Are your tradesmen insured?

Yes. Every tradesman who attends a job is fully insured, and all of our work is guaranteed to industry standards.

How much does a quote cost?

Quotes are free and carry no obligation. Send us the details of the job by phone, WhatsApp or the quote form and we will normally come back to you within the hour.

Do you carry out HAP and Council compliance work?

Yes. We regularly work with HAP landlords, letting agents and local Council compliance jobs, and can carry out the remedial work needed to bring a property up to standard.

Can you handle several different trades on one job?

Yes, that is the point of one number. Plumbing, electrical, roofing, carpentry and decorating are all covered in house, so you do not need to juggle separate contractors on the same property.
